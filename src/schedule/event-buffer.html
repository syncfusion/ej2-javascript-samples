This surgery scheduling demo highlights efficient operating room management using configurable pre-procedure and post-procedure buffer times within the Scheduler. Plan surgeries with dedicated preparation and recovery periods, reduce scheduling overlaps, maximize operating room utilization, and streamline healthcare appointment coordination.

The enableBuffer property of eventSettings activates the buffer feature. The bufferBefore and bufferAfter field mappings (in minutes) are configured through eventSettings.fields, and a custom bufferTemplate renders an icon and the buffer duration inside the buffer pill. Conflict detection with allowOverlap=false also flags the buffered reserved range, and drag-and-drop is restricted to the room matching the event’s required specialty.

Buffers are supported across timeline and vertical time-grid views (Day, Week, WorkWeek, Timeline Day, Timeline Week, Timeline WorkWeek) and re-anchor on the EVENT edges during drag and resize previews.
